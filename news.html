GrantJuly 14, 2026

New NIH R15 Grant Awarded for Cardiotoxicity Research

Dr. Satoru Kobayashi (PI) has been awarded a new NIH Research Enhancement Award (1R15HL179642-01A1) from the National Institutes of Health. The project, titled 'Redefining The Role Of Lysosomal Membrane Permeabilization (LMP) In Doxorubicin-Induced Cardiotoxicity', is funded for $573,750 from July 2026 through June 2029. This research aims to understand the molecular mechanisms of Doxorubicin-induced cardiotoxicity and develop novel protective strategies for cancer patients undergoing chemotherapy.

MissionDecember 31, 2020

Rescue That Frogs! - Our Mission in Research

Our research mission is to save the 'Boiling Frogs.' In diabetes, the heart cells are slowly injured by sugar, but the defense system fails to react, like a frog in boiling water. We study how sugar injures lysosomes—the defense force inside your heart cells—and investigate ways to repair lysosomal injuries to protect your heart.
